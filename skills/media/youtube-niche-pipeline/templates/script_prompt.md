Markup (put each marker on its own line, right before the passage it labels):

- [PATTERN INTERRUPT]: every 350-450 words. A sudden shift: a direct question

  a hard cut to a new scene.
- [EMOTIONAL PEAK]: at least 2. Moments of awe, tension, nostalgia, or vindication.
  Slow the rhythm down going into them.
- [CTA]: once, after the first big payoff (never in the first minute). End with

- Editor directions go in brackets and are not read aloud:
  [B-ROLL: ...], [VISUAL: ...], [SFX: ...], [ON-SCREEN: ...]. Add one every
  100-150 words so the editor always knows what is on screen.

Tone: dramatic and cinematic, like a documentary narrator who is also a fan.
Use short sentences, vary the rhythm, and keep every sentence under 30 words.
Write for the ear: no tables, no bullet lists, no URLs read aloud.

Structure:
1. Hook (0:00-0:15)
2. Context: why this matters right now (the launch is days away / just happened)
3. Three or four escalating beats, each ending in a sub-hook
4. The biggest reveal or best evidence, marked as an emotional peak
5. Counterpoint: what would make this wrong
6. Payoff plus a closing tease
